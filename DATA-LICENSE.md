## 1. What this license covers

"The Data" means the datasets published by Data Centers Near You:

- the CSV files on the [/data/ page](https://willmcnulty.github.io/Virginia-Data-Center-Tracker/data/);
- the JSON files under https://willmcnulty.github.io/Virginia-Data-Center-Tracker/virginia/data/ (facilities.json,
  changes.json, filings.json, meetings.json when published, schools.json, zips.json, meta.json);
- the same information as shown on the site's map and pages.

It includes what this project adds: the selection and arrangement of the records, the joining of air permits to
sites, stages and localities as assigned here, distances, the change log, cleaned and grouped county filings, and any
classification or score this project produces.

## 2. What it doesn't cover

- **The underlying public records**, and text copied from them word for word, such as DEQ facility names and
  addresses, county filing descriptions and plan names, and meeting agenda text. They belong to their publishers and
  keep their publishers' terms. As of October 4, 2026:
  - Virginia DEQ's [Terms of Use](https://geohub-vadeq.hub.arcgis.com/pages/terms-of-use) say its GIS information
    "is in the public domain and may be copied without permission".
  - Loudoun County's [GIS disclaimer](https://logis.loudoun.gov/loudoun/disclaimer.html) limits the county's
    liability and sets no reuse restriction.
  - Data from NCES and the U.S. Census Bureau is a work of the U.S. government and not subject to U.S. copyright.
- **Map tiles** (OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles / OpenStreetMap), which have their own license.
- **The site's code, writing, images, design, name and logo**: see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

Nothing in this license limits what you may do with records you get from their publishers yourself.

      What this shows

      Every site DEQ flags as a data center in its daily Air Sites records. Data centers need a DEQ air permit for their backup diesel generators, so this covers projects from the point they apply for one. The stage (planned, under construction, operating) is DEQ's own.

      Air permit dates come from DEQ's issued-permits list, saved . Sites DEQ has on record but hasn't issued a permit for yet show no date.

      What it doesn't show yet

      • Earlier proposals. A rezoning or special exception is filed with the county before any air permit, so the newest proposals may not be here yet. County records are the next thing to add.
      • Cost and size. These come from county building permits, also next.
      • Meetings and how to take part. Upcoming hearings, comment periods and who decides, by ZIP code, are planned.
